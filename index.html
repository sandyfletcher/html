TOFU BÁNH MÌ
TIP: Draining the sliced tofu thoroughly on paper towels is essential—removing excess surface moisture lets the cornstarch create a delicate, crispy crust without deep-frying.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 (14 oz) block extra-firm tofu, drained and sliced into 8 slabs
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 carrots, peeled and shredded
  • 1 small cucumber, halved lengthwise, seeded, and thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp lime juice + ½ tsp grated lime zest
  • 1 tbsp vegan fish sauce (or soy sauce / tamari)
  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise (or vegan mayo)
  • 1–2 tsp Sriracha
  • ¼ cup cornstarch
  • 3 tbsp high-heat vegetable oil
  • 4 6-inch soft baguette rolls, split lengthwise and toasted
  • ½ cup fresh cilantro leaves

1  Arrange tofu slices in a single layer on a paper towel–lined baking sheet and let drain for 20 minutes. Gently press dry with additional towels, then season with salt and pepper.
2  In a medium bowl, toss shredded carrots, cucumber, lime juice, and vegan fish sauce (or soy sauce); let sit for 15 minutes to quick-pickle. In a separate small bowl, whisk mayonnaise, Sriracha, and lime zest.
3  Spread cornstarch in a shallow dish and dredge seasoned tofu slices, tapping off any excess.
4  Heat oil in a 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add tofu and fry until crisp and golden brown (about 4 minutes per side), then transfer to a paper towel–lined plate.
5  Spread spicy mayonnaise evenly over cut sides of toasted rolls. Layer in crispy tofu slabs, drained pickled vegetables, and fresh cilantro, then close and serve immediately.

STEAM-BOILED
If your eggs are refrigerated, add an extra minute to the steaming time.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 6 eggs

1  Add about an inch of water to a pot and place an expanding steamer ring inside.
2  Place the pot over high heat, cover, and bring the water to a rolling boil.
3  Carefully place the eggs into the steamer basket - or directly into the water if you don't have one - then cover and reduce heat to medium-high.
4  Cook for 6½ mins for a runny yolk, 8 mins for jammy yolk, or 12 mins for firm.
5  Immediately transfer eggs to an ice bath for 5 minutes to stop the cooking process, then peel and serve.

DEVILED
Older eggs peel easier than fresh ones. Try to buy your eggs a week in advance.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 12 eggs
  • 3 tsp dijon mustard
  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp minced shallot / onion
  • 1 tsp sugar, ⅛ tsp salt, ⅛ tsp pepper
  • ¼ tsp tabasco sauce
  • Paprika, chives, pickle juice (for garnish)

1  Place eggs in a large saucepan and cover with water. Transfer to stovetop over high heat until water begins to boil.
2  Boil for one minute, cover with lid, and remove from heat. Allow to sit for 17 minutes and then drain and transfer to an ice bath.
3  Peel eggs and set aside. Slice each egg in half lengthwise and gently remove yolk halves into small mixing bowl. Arrange egg whites on serving platter.
4  Use a fork to mash the yolks together with mustard, mayonnaise, butter, lemon juice, shallot/onion, sugar, salt, pepper, and tabasco.
5  Add mayonnaise as necessary to get desired creamy consistency, then pipe or spoon mixture back into egg halves. Sprinkle with paprika and chives.

BREAKFAST BURRITO
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • eggs
  • ground turkey
  • cottage cheese
  • large tortillas
  • kale, chopped
  • chili powder
  • paprika
  • onion powder
  • garlic powder
  • cumin
  • oregano
  • red pepper flakes
  • black pepper
  • salt

1  Heat a pan with a little oil or cooking spray over medium-high heat. Add ground turkey and cook, breaking it apart, until browned.
2  Sprinkle generously with your desired combination of spices (chili powder, paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, cumin, oregano, red pepper flakes, black pepper, salt). Stir to combine.
3  Once turkey is browned and seasoned, add cottage cheese to the pan. Stir and combine, allowing the cottage cheese to melt down and mix with the turkey.
4  Add chopped kale to the mixture. Stir and cook until the kale is wilted. Remove the turkey-kale mixture from the heat and set aside.
5  Return the pan to the heat (add a little more oil if needed). Cook eggs to your preference (scrambled is common for burritos).
6  Warm the tortillas slightly to make them pliable.
7  Assemble burritos: Spoon a portion of the meat/cheese/kale mixture and a portion of the cooked eggs onto each tortilla.
8  Fold in the sides of the tortilla, then roll up tightly from the bottom to form a burrito.
9  If storing for later, wrap each burrito individually in parchment paper and then tinfoil. These can be refrigerated or frozen. Reheat in oven, microwave, or air fryer.

CRUSTY NO-KNEAD
If you don't have a Dutch Oven, you'll lose the trapped steam and the delicious crust. Still edible!

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 tsp active dry yeast
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1½ cups very warm tap water
  • 3 cups bread flour

1  Whisk yeast and salt in a large bowl with warm water.
2  Add in all the flour at once and stir until a sticky dough forms.
3  Cover with a kitchen towel and leave for 2-3 hours to rise until doubled in size.
4  Preheat oven to 450°F with Dutch oven inside and lid on.
5  Dust dough with flour and form it into a ball on parchment paper.
6  Lift paper and dough into Dutch oven and return to oven.
7  Bake 30 minutes covered, then 12 minutes uncovered.
8  Remove and place on cooling rack for at least 10 minutes before slicing.

PIZZA DOUGH
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 cup water, warmed
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt

1  Combine water, yeast, and sugar in medium bowl. Let stand until the mixture foams on top - about 5 minutes.
2  In the meantime, combine flour and salt. Once yeast mixture is foamy, mix it into the flour mixture.
3  Knead combination on floured surface, then place in lightly oiled bowl and cover with a clean cloth.
4  Let the dough rise - about 30 minutes - then cut the dough in half.
5  Use or bag each portion for later use.

POTATO SALAD
TIP: Allow potatoes to cool slightly before adding mayonnaise to prevent it from splitting and becoming oily.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1½ cups yellow potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 cup chopped snap peas or green beans
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • Fresh dill, chopped (to taste)
  • Black pepper (to taste)

1  Cover potatoes with water in a large pot, add salt.
2  Cover and bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to medium and simmer uncovered until fork-tender (10–12 minutes).
3  Drain potatoes and return to pot (off heat).
4  Add mustard, mayonnaise, snap peas/green beans, green onions and dill. Season with pepper.
5  Stir to coat and combine. Serve warm or chilled.

ROAST POTATOES
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 liters water
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 4 lb Yukon Gold or Russet potatoes, peeled/chunked
  • 5 tbsp olive oil or animal fat
  • Small handful chopped fresh rosemary leaves
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Black pepper
  • Small handful minced fresh parsley leaves

1  Preheat oven to 450°F.
2  Boil water in a large pot, then add salt, baking soda and potatoes.
3  Return to boil, reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes or until potatoes are easily pierced with a knife.
4  In a saucepan, combine oil/fat, rosemary, garlic and pepper. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until garlic is golden (about 3 minutes).
5  Strain oil through a fine mesh into a large bowl, retaining both parts (herbs/garlic solids and infused oil).
6  Drain potatoes well and let rest in the pot for a minute for moisture to evaporate.
7  Transfer potatoes to the large bowl with the infused oil, season with salt and pepper, and shake roughly to coat and create a thick starchy paste on the chunks.
8  Spread potatoes evenly on a baking sheet and roast undisturbed for 20 minutes.
9  Remove from oven, turn potatoes with a thin spatula and continue roasting for 30–35 minutes, turning and shaking occasionally, until deeply golden and crisp.
10  Transfer potatoes to a serving bowl, add the reserved herbs/garlic solids and fresh parsley. Toss to coat and serve immediately.

POTATO DAUPHINOISE
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2+ cups Gruyère cheese, grated
  • 2½ lb Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups warmed heavy cream
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • ½ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 2 dried bay leaves
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves

1  Preheat oven to 350°F with rack in the middle.
2  Coat a 4-quart baking dish with butter.
3  Slice potatoes into ¼-inch thick rounds and place in a large pot.
4  Add garlic, cream, milk, salt, pepper, nutmeg and bay leaves. Simmer over medium heat, stirring often, until potatoes are fork-tender (5–7 minutes).
5  Remove bay leaves.
6  Transfer half the potatoes to the baking dish, arranging in an even layer. Top with half the grated cheese.
7  Arrange remaining potatoes on top, then pour enough cream mixture to reach just below the top layer.
8  Top with remaining cheese and bake until golden-brown and easily pierced (about 45 minutes).
9  Garnish with thyme and let rest 15 minutes on a wire rack before serving.

BAKED FRIES
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1½ lb potatoes
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp dill weed
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp Parmesan cheese
  • Optional: cayenne, chili powder or mustard powder (to taste)

1  Preheat oven to 425°F.
2  Wash and cut potatoes into fry shapes (e.g., ¼ to ½ inch thick). For crispier fries, soak in cold water for at least 30 minutes, then drain and pat thoroughly dry with paper towels.
3  In a large bowl, toss potatoes with oil until evenly coated.
4  Spread potatoes in a single layer on a large baking sheet (or two if necessary to avoid overcrowding).
5  Bake for 20–30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown and crispy. Cooking time will vary based on thickness.
6  While fries are baking, combine fresh parsley, kosher salt, pepper, garlic powder, dill weed, onion powder, and Parmesan cheese in a small bowl. Add any optional spices if desired.
7  Once fries are cooked, immediately transfer them to a large bowl, sprinkle with the seasoning mixture, and toss to coat evenly.
8  Serve hot.

ROASTED TOMATO
Substitute coconut milk to make it vegan!

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • dozen tomatoes, halved
  • large knob ginger
  • 2 heads garlic, unpeeled
  • red onion
  • bell pepper
  • carrot
  • 3 cups vegetable stock
  • balsamic vinegar
  • heavy cream
  • fresh basil

1  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2  Place halved tomatoes, onion, bell pepper, garlic, and carrot on a baking sheet.
3  Drizzle everything generously with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
4  Bake for 35-40 minutes until roasted and slightly charred.
5  Squeeze the roasted garlic cloves out of their skins and discard the skins.
6  Scrape the pan to collect all juices and flavor bits, then transfer all roasted vegetables to a pot or blending container.
7  Add a splash of balsamic vinegar and fresh basil.
8  Stir in a splash of heavy cream, and season to taste.

BROCCOLI CHEDDAR
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 tbsp butter (for onions) + 4 tbsp butter (for roux), divided
  • 1 yellow onion, diced thin
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 2 cups cream, warmed
  • 3 cups broccoli, chopped thin
  • 2 carrots, sliced thin
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¾ tsp pepper
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp dry mustard
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 8 oz sharp cheddar, grated

1  Sauté the onion and 1 tbsp of butter in a saucepan over medium heat until onions become translucent, adding garlic near the end. Remove from heat and set aside.
2  In a large pot, melt the remaining 4 tbsp of butter. Whisk in flour and cook over medium heat for 3–4 minutes, stirring constantly, to create a thick roux.
3  Slowly whisk in chicken stock, ensuring no lumps form. Then, slowly whisk in warmed cream.
4  Simmer over low heat for 15–20 minutes to thicken, whisking occasionally and incorporating any skin that forms on top.
5  Stir in the sautéed onion-garlic mixture, chopped broccoli, sliced carrots, salt, pepper, paprika, dry mustard, and cayenne pepper.
6  Simmer for 20–25 minutes, or until vegetables are tender, whisking occasionally.
7  Remove from heat. Stir in grated cheddar cheese until fully melted and smooth, about 1 minute.
8  Serve hot.

GOLDEN CHICKEN NOODLE
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 tbsp canola oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 8 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 bunch scallions, thinly sliced and separated by color (whites and greens)
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Sea salt to taste
  • 2 skin-on, bone-in chicken breasts
  • ¼ lb thin round rice noodles, broken into inch-long pieces
  • 4 stalks celery, chopped
  • ⅓ cup fresh dill, finely chopped

1  Heat oil in a large Dutch oven or pot over medium-high heat.
2  Add onion, ginger, garlic, and scallion whites. Cook until softened, about 3 minutes.
3  Add turmeric and black pepper, stirring until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
4  Add water and bay leaf, lightly seasoning with sea salt.
5  Bring to a simmer, then add chicken breasts and reduce heat to low.
6  Cover and cook until chicken is cooked through, about 20 minutes.
7  Transfer chicken to a cutting board and let rest until cool enough to handle.
8  Remove and discard skin and bones, then shred the meat into bite-sized pieces.
9  Raise heat under the pot to medium and add noodles.
10  Cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until noodles are springy but still slightly firm, about 8 minutes (or according to package directions).
11  Return shredded chicken to the pot along with chopped celery. Cook until celery is bright green and tender-crisp, and noodles are fully cooked, about 4 minutes more.
12  Remove from heat and stir in chopped dill and half the scallion greens.
13  Serve hot, garnished with remaining scallion greens on top.

GOLDEN GET-WELL
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 10 scallions
  • 10 garlic cloves
  • 1 (4-inch) piece fresh ginger
  • 2 limes
  • 1½ lb bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 4 thighs)
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • 1¼ tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tbsp Kosher salt (plus more to taste)
  • ¾ cup jasmine or basmati rice
  • 9 cups water
  • 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper (plus more for garnish)
  • Cilantro leaves and tender stems (for garnish)

1  Do some prep:
  • Thinly slice 10 scallions crosswise, setting aside about ½ cup of the dark green parts for serving.
  • Lightly smash and peel 10 garlic cloves.
  • Thinly slice 1 (4-inch) piece of ginger lengthwise into planks. No need to peel it! Just give it a scrub with some water if it looks dirty.
2  Start the soup:
  • Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the scallions (white and light green parts), garlic, ginger, and 1¼ tsp turmeric. Cook, stirring often, until the aromatics are softened and fragrant but not browned, 3 to 4 minutes.
  • Add 1½ lb bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, 1 tbsp salt, 9 cups water, and ¾ cup jasmine rice to the pot. (Note: In most cases, it's best to first rinse the rice to remove any excess starch that would cause the rice to stick together as it cooks, but since we're aiming for thickened porridge, there's no need to rinse it.) Bring the water to a simmer over medium-high heat. Cook, scraping the bottom of the pot if the rice is sticking and reducing the heat as necessary to maintain a simmer, until the rice breaks down and thickens the soup to a porridge-like consistency, 40 to 50 minutes.
3  Shred the chicken:
  • Using tongs, pluck out the chicken and transfer it to a plate to cool.
  • While the chicken cools, use a wooden spoon to fish around for the garlic cloves, which at this point will be very, very soft. Use the back of the spoon to smash the garlic against the inside walls of the pot to crush and incorporate it into the soup. If you feel like it, you can pluck out and discard the sliced ginger at this point as well—it’s a little tough to eat, but I usually just eat around it.
  • Once the chicken has cooled, remove and discard the skin. Use two forks to shred the meat from the bones. Discard the bones. Return the shredded meat to the pot and give it a stir.
4  Season the soup and serve:
  • Squeeze the juice of 2 limes into the soup.
  • Stir in 1 tsp black pepper. Taste the soup and add more salt if you think it needs it.
  • Divide the soup among soup bowls. Garnish it with the reserved sliced scallion greens, some cilantro leaves, a good crank of black pepper, and an additional drizzle of olive oil.

CHICKEN TORTELLINI
If you're planning on leftovers, cook the tortellini separately and add it to each bowl so it doesn't go soggy.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 lb boneless/skinless chicken thigh
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp dried basil
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 28 oz can diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups tortellini (fresh or frozen)
  • 3 cups spinach
  • ½ cup parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 cup cream, warmed

1  Season chicken with salt and pepper and let refrigerate while preparing other ingredients.
2  In a skillet, heat olive oil. Fry onions and garlic until translucent. Stir in flour, basil, and tomato paste; cook for 1 minute. Transfer this mixture to the slow cooker.
3  Stir chicken broth, diced tomatoes, and seasoned chicken into the slow cooker.
4  Cover and cook on LOW for 4–6 hours, or HIGH for 3–4 hours, until chicken is tender.
5  Remove chicken from slow cooker and shred. Return shredded chicken to the slow cooker.
6  Stir in tortellini, parmesan cheese, and warmed cream.
7  Cook on HIGH for 10 more minutes, or until tortellini are cooked through (according to package directions if different). Add spinach during the last few minutes of cooking, just until wilted.
8  Serve hot.

BUTTERNUT SQUASH
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • 1 (3 lb) butternut squash, peeled, seeded, cubed
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • ½ tbsp fresh rosemary, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3–4 cups vegetable broth
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Chopped parsley (optional, for serving)
  • Toasted pepitas (pumpkin seeds) (optional, for serving)
  • Crusty bread (optional, for serving)

1  Sauté: In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add onion, sea salt, and a few grinds of black pepper. Cook for 5–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and translucent.
2  Add squash: Stir in cubed butternut squash and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it begins to soften and lightly caramelize.
3  Add aromatics: Add chopped garlic, sage, rosemary, and grated ginger. Cook for 30 seconds to 1 minute more, until fragrant.
4  Simmer: Pour in 3 cups of vegetable broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20–30 minutes, or until the squash is very tender.
5  Blend: Let the soup cool slightly. Carefully transfer in batches to a blender and blend until smooth and creamy (or use an immersion blender directly in the pot). Add more broth if needed to reach your desired consistency.
6  Serve: Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper if necessary. Ladle into bowls and garnish with chopped parsley and toasted pepitas, if using. Serve with crusty bread on the side.

RATATOUILLE
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 medium-large eggplant, 1 lb, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • Sea salt
  • 6 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more as needed
  • 2 medium zucchini, 1 lb, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 red or yellow bell pepper, stemmed, seeded, and cut into ½-inch pieces
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 lb tomatoes on the vine, 3 to 5, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • Pinch red pepper flakes
  • Pinch cane sugar
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 2 tsp chopped fresh thyme leaves
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • ¼ cup thinly sliced fresh basil leaves, plus more torn basil for garnish

1  Place the eggplant in a colander and sprinkle with ½ tsp salt. Place in the sink or over a large rimmed plate and set aside for 20 minutes to drain. Blot dry with a clean kitchen towel or paper towels.
2  Heat 3 tbsp of the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the eggplant and cook, stirring occasionally, for 10 to 15 minutes, or until tender. Transfer to a large bowl and set aside.
3  Add another 1 tbsp olive oil and the zucchini to the skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 3 to 5 minutes, or until the zucchini is tender but not mushy. Season with ¼ tsp salt and transfer to the bowl with the eggplant.
4  Add another 1 tbsp olive oil to the pan. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, or until softened. Stir in the garlic and ¼ tsp salt and cook for another 10 minutes, or until very tender. If the pan becomes dry, add more oil as needed.
5  Stir the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil into the peppers and onions. Add the tomatoes, red pepper flakes, and sugar and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, or until the tomatoes break down.
6  Add the eggplant and zucchini back into the skillet along with the vinegar, thyme, ¼ tsp salt, and several grinds of pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 10 to 15 minutes, or until the ratatouille is thick. Stir in the basil and season to taste. Garnish with more basil and serve.

CREAM OF ASPARAGUS
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 2 lb asparagus, ends trimmed, chopped
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • ½ cup heavy cream, warmed
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Chives or dill for garnish (optional)

1  Melt butter in a large pot over medium heat.
2  Add garlic, cooking until fragrant (about 1 minute).
3  Add chopped asparagus, seasoning with salt and pepper. Cook until asparagus is tender-crisp and bright green (about 5 minutes).
4  Add chicken broth and bring to a simmer. Cover and cook until asparagus is very tender (10–15 minutes).

6  Return the pureed soup to the pot if using a countertop blender. Stir in the warmed heavy cream.
7  Heat gently on low. Do not allow it to boil after adding cream.
8  Season with additional salt and pepper to taste.
9  Serve hot, garnished with fresh chives or dill, if desired.

KIMCHI
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 10 lb baechu (napa cabbage)
  • 1 cup kosher salt
PORRIDGE
  • ½ cup sweet rice flour
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 3 cups water
PASTE
  • 1 cup crushed garlic
  • 1–2 tbsp minced ginger
  • 1 cup minced onion
  • 1 cup fish sauce
  • salty fermented squid (optional)
  • 2½ cups Korean hot pepper flakes (gochugaru), adjust to taste
  • 2 cups chopped leek
  • 10 green onions (diagonally sliced)
  • ¼ cup julienned carrot
  • 2 cups julienned Korean radish

1  Prepare Cabbage: Trim discolored outer leaves from napa cabbage. Cut cabbage lengthwise into quarters, remove cores, and chop into bite-sized pieces.
2  Soak cabbage pieces in cold water, then drain and place into a large basin. Sprinkle with 1 cup kosher salt, tossing to distribute.
3  Turn cabbage every 30 minutes for a total of 1½ hours to ensure even salting.
4  Rinse the cabbage thoroughly in cold water 3 times to remove excess salt. Drain well and set aside.
5  Make Porridge: In a pot, combine 3 cups water and ½ cup sweet rice flour. Mix well and bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Once it bubbles (about 5 minutes), add ¼ cup sugar. Stir and cook for a few more minutes until translucent. Let it cool down completely.
6  Make Kimchi Paste: Place the cooled porridge into a very large mixing bowl. Add fish sauce, hot pepper flakes (gochugaru), crushed garlic, minced ginger, and minced onion. If using, add chopped salty fermented squid. Mix well.
7  Add chopped leek, diagonally sliced green onions, julienned carrot, and julienned Korean radish to the paste. Mix all ingredients thoroughly.
8  Combine: Add the drained, salted cabbage to the kimchi paste in the large basin. Mix by hand (preferably wearing gloves) until the cabbage is evenly coated with the paste. If your basin isn't large enough, do this in batches.
9  Store & Ferment: Transfer the kimchi to an airtight container or glass jars, pressing down to remove air pockets.
10  You can eat it fresh immediately, or allow it to ferment. For fermentation, you can leave it at room temperature for 1–2 days (taste daily) until it reaches your desired sourness, then refrigerate to slow down fermentation. Alternatively, refrigerate immediately for slower fermentation.

PINEAPPLE GUACAMOLE
TIP: Don't skip the grilling! Caramelized pineapple adds depth that raw fruit lacks.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 4 discs fresh pineapple, cored (about ½ inch thick)
PINEAPPLE GLAZE
  • 1 lime, juiced (reserve some for guacamole)
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1½ tsp chili powder (for glaze)
GUACAMOLE
  • 4 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted
  • Remaining lime juice
  • 1½ cups finely chopped red onion
  • ½ cup diced tomato
  • 1 minced jalapeño (seeds removed for less heat)
  • 1 clove minced garlic
  • ½ tsp coarse sea salt or kosher salt
  • ¼ cup finely chopped fresh cilantro leaves
  • Dash of cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder (for garnish, optional)
  • Oil for grill/pan

1  Prepare Pineapple: Combine juice of one lime (or part of it, reserving some for guac), brown sugar, honey, and 1½ tsp chili powder in a bowl to make the glaze.
2  Heat a grill pan or outdoor grill to medium-high heat. Lightly oil the grates/pan.
3  Brush pineapple discs with the glaze. Cook for 3–4 minutes per side, or until lightly charred and caramelized, basting with excess glaze occasionally.
4  Remove pineapple from heat and let cool, then dice into small pieces.
5  Make Guacamole: In a large bowl, mash avocados to desired consistency (chunky or smooth). Stir in the remaining lime juice.
6  Add diced red onion, diced tomato, minced jalapeño, minced garlic, coarse salt, chopped cilantro, and a dash of cumin. Mix thoroughly.
7  Gently fold in the charred, diced pineapple.
8  Transfer to a serving bowl. If desired, sprinkle the top with the additional 1 tsp chili powder for garnish.
9  Serve immediately with tortilla chips.

SPICE RICE
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 small red pepper, diced
  • ½ tsp chili flakes
  • 2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2½ cups chicken stock
  • ½ cup frozen peas
  • 1½ cups long-grain rice (e.g. Basmati or Jasmine)

1  Heat olive oil in a large saucepan or pot with a lid over medium heat.
2  Add diced onion and red pepper. Cook, stirring constantly, until tender (about 5–7 minutes).
3  Add chili flakes, turmeric, cumin, and paprika. Stir and cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
4  Add chicken stock and rice. Bring the mixture to a boil.
5  Once boiling, cover the pot with a lid, reduce heat to low, and cook for 7 minutes.
6  Stir the rice, then add the frozen peas. Re-cover and continue cooking for another 7 minutes, or until all liquid is absorbed and rice is tender. Stir occasionally if needed to prevent sticking.
7  Uncover, fluff with a fork, and serve hot.

PICO DE GALLO

  • ½ cup chopped cilantro
  • 2 tbsp lime juice (from 1 fresh lime)

ENCHILADAS
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 red onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 30 oz canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 medium tomato, diced
  • 4 oz canned chopped green chilies, undrained (e.g., one small can)
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 cup corn (frozen or canned, drained)
  • 8–10 tortillas (corn or flour)
  • 1 can (approx. 15 oz) refried beans
  • 1 cup enchilada sauce
  • 4 oz (1 cup) shredded cheese (e.g., cheddar, Monterey Jack)
  • Salt and pepper (to taste)

1  Preheat oven to 375°F. Lightly grease a 9×13 inch baking dish.
2  Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
3  Add chopped red onion and garlic; cook for about 5 minutes, until softened.
4  Add drained black beans, diced tomato, undrained green chilies, chili powder, cumin, and soy sauce. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer.
5  Lower heat, cover, and simmer for 10 minutes.
6  Stir in corn. Uncover and simmer for 5 minutes more, allowing some liquid to evaporate.
7  Season the bean mixture with salt and pepper to taste.
8  Warm tortillas slightly to make them pliable. Spread a thin layer of refried beans on one side of each tortilla.
9  Spoon some of the bean and vegetable mixture down the center of each tortilla. Roll them up and place seam-down in the prepared baking dish.
10  Pour enchilada sauce evenly over the rolled tortillas. Sprinkle with shredded cheese.
11  Bake for 20 minutes, or until heated through and cheese is melted and bubbly. Let stand for a few minutes before serving.

BAKED FETA
Use a block of feta in brine rather than a crumbled product, which contains anti-caking agents that prevent it from melting smoothly.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 pints cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 8 oz block feta cheese
  • ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 10 ozdry pasta
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves, roughly chopped

1  Preheat oven to 400°F.
2  Toss cherry tomatoes in a 9x13" baking dish with oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, pepper, and most of the olive oil until well-coated.
3  Nestle block of feta into center of baking dish and drizzle with remaining olive oil.
4  Bake for 30 minutes, then increase heat to 450°F for 5-10 minutes until tomatoes burst and feta is golden-brown.
5  While baking, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook pasta until al dente, reserving ½ cup of pasta water while draining.
6  Immediately add garlic to hot baking dish, then mash feta and tomatoes with fork in to form a sauce.
7  Incorporate cooked pasta and fresh basil into sauce, splashing in reserved pasta water as needed until glossy and evenly coated.

LEMON FETTUCCINE ALFREDO

  • 1 lb fettuccine
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1+ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • Optional: Brie cheese, cubed (amount to taste)

1  Whisk heavy cream, egg yolks, parmesan cheese, and lemon zest in a bowl. Season with salt and pepper. Set aside.

5  Stir in the lemon juice. If using Brie, add it now and stir until melted and incorporated.
6  Taste and adjust seasoning with more salt, pepper, or lemon juice if needed.
7  Serve immediately, garnished with additional grated parmesan cheese.

SPAGHETTI BOLOGNESE
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped (brown, yellow or white)
  • 1 lb beef mince (ground beef), or half pork and half beef
  • ½ cup dry red wine (can substitute with water or beef broth/stock)
  • 2 beef bouillon cubes, crumbled (or 2 tsp granulated beef bouillon)
  • 28 oz can crushed tomatoes (or tomato passata)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp white sugar (if needed, to balance acidity of tomatoes)
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 dried bay leaves
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme (or ½ tsp dried thyme or oregano)
  • ¾ tsp cooking salt (kosher salt)
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 13 oz spaghetti, dried
  • Parmesan cheese, for serving (optional)
  • Finely chopped fresh parsley, for garnish (optional)

1  Heat olive oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add onion and garlic; cook for 3 minutes, or until light golden and softened.
2  Increase heat to high and add beef mince. Cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned.
3  Pour in red wine. Bring to a simmer and cook for 1 minute, scraping any browned bits from the bottom of the pot, until the alcohol smell has evaporated.
4  Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, crumbled beef bouillon cubes, sugar (if needed), Worcestershire sauce, bay leaves, thyme, cooking salt, and black pepper.
5  Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low. Cook uncovered for at least 20–30 minutes (or up to 1 hour for deeper flavor), stirring occasionally. If the sauce becomes too thick, add a splash of water. (Optional richer sauce: Add ¾ cup of water, cover, and simmer on very low for 2–2.5 hours, stirring every 30 minutes. Uncover and simmer for another 20 minutes to thicken.)
6  Taste the sauce and adjust seasoning if necessary (more salt, pepper, or sugar). Remove bay leaves and thyme sprigs (if fresh sprigs were used).
7  While the sauce simmers, cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente. Drain well.
8  Serve the bolognese sauce over the cooked spaghetti. Garnish with Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley, if desired.
9  (Optional "Restaurant Style" Tossing Method): After draining spaghetti (reserve 1 cup of pasta water), add the pasta directly to the pot with the bolognese sauce. Add about ½ cup of the reserved pasta water. Toss gently over medium heat for 1–2 minutes, until the spaghetti is well-coated and the sauce has emulsified. Serve immediately, garnished as desired.

BAKED MACARONI AND CHEESE
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 lb cavatappi pasta
  • 16 oz mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 16 oz Colby Jack cheese, grated
  • 8 oz Cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 (12-oz) can evaporated milk
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard

1  Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.
2  Cook cavatappi according to package directions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
3  In a large bowl, combine the grated mozzarella, Colby Jack, and Cheddar cheeses. Divide this mixture in half; set one half aside for layering and topping.
4  In a small bowl, combine the garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Divide this seasoning mixture in half and set aside.
5  Melt butter in a large skillet or saucepan over medium heat.
6  Once melted, stir in half of the seasoning mixture and the flour. Cook, stirring constantly, for 3 to 5 minutes to form a smooth paste (roux).
7  Gradually whisk in the evaporated milk until the sauce begins to thicken.
8  Stir in the heavy cream and the remaining half of the seasoning mixture. Mix until well combined.
9  Whisk in the Dijon mustard until the sauce is thick and smooth.
10  Reduce heat to low. Add one half of the total cheese mixture (the portion not set aside for layering) to the sauce, a handful at a time, stirring until each addition is melted before adding the next, creating a smooth cheese sauce.
11  Add the cooked cavatappi to the cheese sauce and stir until well coated.
12  Spread half of the macaroni and cheese mixture into the prepared baking dish.
13  Sprinkle half of the reserved shredded cheese (the portion set aside in step 3) over the macaroni layer.
14  Top with the remaining macaroni and cheese mixture.
15  Sprinkle the remaining reserved shredded cheese over the top.
16  Bake in the preheated oven for 25 to 30 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
17  For a golden-brown top, switch the oven to broil and broil for about 2 minutes, watching carefully to prevent burning. Let cool slightly before serving.

BUTTER CHICKEN
NOTE: Do not let the sauce boil after adding the sour cream/dairy at the end to prevent curdling.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 onions, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • ¾ tsp ground coriander
  • ¾ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 can (14–15 oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • ½ cup unsweetened peanut butter (creamy or chunky)
  • 3 lb skinless, boneless chicken thighs, cut into 1–2 inch pieces
  • 1 cup sour cream (or plain yogurt/heavy cream)
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • Cooked rice or naan bread (for serving)

1  Combine diced onions, minced garlic, butter, grated ginger, brown sugar, chili powder, coriander, turmeric, cinnamon, cumin, salt, pepper, and undrained diced tomatoes in the slow cooker.
2  In a separate bowl, whisk chicken broth with peanut butter until smooth. Pour this mixture into the slow cooker and stir everything to combine.
3  Cover and cook on LOW for 5–8 hours or on HIGH for 3–4 hours.
4  Once the sauce ingredients have cooked, use an immersion blender to purée the mixture directly in the slow cooker until smooth. (Alternatively, carefully transfer in batches to a regular blender and blend, then return to slow cooker).
5  Add the chicken pieces to the puréed sauce in the slow cooker. Stir to coat.
6  Cover and cook on HIGH for another 30–60 minutes, or on LOW for 1–2 hours, until chicken is cooked through and tender.
7  Stir in sour cream (or alternative) and chopped cilantro just before serving. Do not let it boil after adding sour cream.
8  Serve hot over cooked rice or with naan bread.

CHICKEN PARMESAN
 I N G R E D I E N T S  MARINARA SAUCE
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 6 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 (28-oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 large sprigs basil
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
CHICKEN
  • 1 cup panko bread crumbs
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 cups finely grated Parmesan, divided
  • ¾ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 6 (¼-inch-thick) boneless, skinless chicken cutlets (1½ lb total)
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1 cup vegetable or canola oil (for frying)
  • Chopped fresh basil (for serving)

1  Marinara Sauce: Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Stir in garlic, reduce heat to low. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, for 10 minutes. Uncover and cook another 5 minutes, until very soft. Stir in crushed tomatoes and basil sprigs. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for about 30 minutes, or until sauce has thickened. Discard basil sprigs. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
2  Chicken: Preheat broiler. Set a wire rack on a baking sheet.
3  Prepare dredging stations:
  • In a shallow dish: flour.
  • In another shallow dish: whisk eggs with 1 tbsp water.
  • In a third shallow dish: combine panko bread crumbs, 1 tsp garlic powder, and 1 cup grated Parmesan.
  • In a separate bowl: combine shredded mozzarella and the remaining 1 cup grated Parmesan for topping.
4  If chicken cutlets are uneven, pound them to an even ¼-inch thickness. Pat chicken dry and season with Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper on both sides.
5  Dip each chicken cutlet first into flour (shaking off excess), then into the egg mixture (letting excess drip off), and finally into the panko-Parmesan mixture, pressing gently to adhere.
6  In a large, heavy-bottomed skillet, heat vegetable or canola oil over medium-high heat until it reaches 350°F or shimmers. (About ½ inch of oil).
7  Working in batches, fry chicken for 1–2 minutes per side, until golden brown and cooked through. Do not overcrowd the pan.
8  Transfer cooked chicken to the prepared wire rack. Repeat with remaining chicken.
9  Spoon a generous amount of marinara sauce over each chicken cutlet. Sprinkle with the mozzarella-Parmesan cheese mixture.
10  Broil until cheese is melted, bubbly, and has some golden brown spots, about 2–3 minutes. Watch carefully to prevent burning.
11  Top with chopped fresh basil and serve immediately, with extra marinara sauce on the side if desired.

CHICKEN POT PIE
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 lb skinless, boneless chicken breast halves, cubed
  • 1 cup sliced carrots
  • 1 cup frozen green peas
  • ½ cup sliced celery
  • ⅓ cup butter
  • ⅓ cup chopped onion
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp celery seed
  • 1¾ cups chicken broth
  • ⅔ cup milk
  • 2 (9-inch) unbaked pie crusts (store-bought or homemade)

1  Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2  In a saucepan, combine cubed chicken, sliced carrots, frozen peas, and sliced celery. Add water to cover and bring to a boil. Boil for 15 minutes, then remove from the heat and drain. Set aside.
3  While chicken and vegetables are cooking, melt butter in another saucepan over medium heat. Add chopped onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 5–7 minutes.
4  Stir in flour, salt, pepper, and celery seed. Cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
5  Slowly stir in chicken broth and milk.
6  Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring frequently, until the sauce has thickened, about 5–10 minutes. Remove from heat.
7  Place one unbaked pie crust in the bottom of a 9-inch pie plate.
8  Add the cooked chicken and vegetable mixture to the bottom pie crust. Pour the hot broth mixture over the chicken and vegetables.
9  Cover with the top crust. Seal the edges by crimping them together. Cut away any excess dough. Make several small slits in the top crust to allow steam to escape.
10  Bake in the preheated oven until the pastry is golden brown and the filling is bubbly, about 30–35 minutes.
11  Let cool for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!

PAD KRAPOW GAI
 I N G R E D I E N T S  SAUCE
  • 2 tbsp Thai fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp Thai oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
STIR-FRY
  • 6 tbsp canola oil (divided)
  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 red bird's-eye chili, finely chopped (adjust to heat preference)
  • 1 long red chili (e.g., Fresno or Holland), halved, seeded, thinly sliced
  • 200g green beans, cut on bias into ¼-inch pieces
  • 1½ cups loosely packed Thai basil leaves (Holy basil preferred, sweet basil acceptable)
SERVING
  • 4 large eggs
  • Jasmine rice, cooked
  • Lime wedges

1  Prepare Sauce: Stir fish sauce, oyster sauce, soy sauce, water, and brown sugar in a small bowl until sugar is dissolved. Set aside.
2  Cook Chicken: Heat 3 tbsp of oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat until shimmering. Add ground chicken and cook, breaking up the meat with a spoon, until liquid has evaporated and chicken is starting to brown, about 5–7 minutes.
3  Reduce heat to medium. Add minced garlic and chopped chilies (bird's-eye and long red). Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
4  Add green beans and the prepared fish sauce mixture. Stir constantly until the sauce coats the chicken and green beans are tender-crisp, about 2–3 minutes.
5  Remove from heat and stir in Thai basil leaves until just wilted. Cover and set aside.
6  Fry Eggs: Heat the remaining 3 tbsp of oil in a small non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Cook eggs one at a time (or two if pan is large enough), spooning hot oil over the whites, until edges are browned and crispy and yolks remain runny, about 1–2 minutes.
7  Serve: Serve the Thai basil chicken stir-fry over steamed jasmine rice, topped with a fried egg. Offer lime wedges on the side.

TUSCAN CHICKEN
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 6–8 oz each)
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1½ cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • Lemon wedges (for serving)

1  Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
2  Season chicken breasts on both sides with salt, pepper, and dried oregano. Add chicken to the hot skillet and cook until golden brown and cooked through, about 6–8 minutes per side (internal temperature should reach 165°F). Remove chicken from skillet and set aside.
3  In the same skillet over medium heat, melt butter. Stir in minced garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
4  Add halved cherry tomatoes and season with a pinch of salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tomatoes are beginning to burst and soften, about 5–7 minutes.
5  Add baby spinach to the skillet and cook, stirring, until spinach is wilted, about 2–3 minutes.
6  Stir in heavy cream and Parmesan cheese. Bring the mixture to a simmer. Reduce heat to low and simmer until the sauce has slightly reduced and thickened, about 3–5 minutes.
7  Return the cooked chicken breasts to the skillet, nestling them into the sauce. Cook until chicken is heated through, about 5–7 minutes, spooning some of the sauce over the chicken.
8  Serve immediately, with lemon wedges on the side for squeezing over the chicken.

BAKED CHICKEN BREAST
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts, pounded to even thickness (approx. ½ - ¾ inch)
  • 1 tbsp melted butter or olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt (for seasoning)
  • ½ tsp freshly-cracked black pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
BRINE (Optional)
  • 4 cups warm water
  • ¼ cup kosher salt

1  Brine (Optional but Recommended): In a large bowl, dissolve ¼ cup kosher salt in 1 L of warm water. Add the chicken breasts and let them brine for 15–30 minutes at room temperature. (Alternatively, cover and refrigerate for up to 6 hours). Remove chicken breasts from brine, rinse thoroughly with cold water to remove excess salt, then pat them completely dry with paper towels.
2  Preheat oven to 450°F.
3  Place the chicken breasts in a single layer in a baking dish or on a baking sheet. Brush both sides of the chicken with melted butter or olive oil.
4  In a small bowl, whisk together 1 tsp kosher salt (use less if you brined for a long time), black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika. Sprinkle this seasoning mixture evenly over both sides of the chicken breasts.
5  Bake for 15–20 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the internal temperature reaches 165°F at the thickest part. Cooking time will vary depending on breast thickness.
6  For a more golden top, you can turn the broiler on high for the final 2–3 minutes of cooking. Watch carefully to prevent burning.
7  Remove the pan from the oven. Transfer the chicken to a clean cutting board or plate, tent loosely with foil, and let rest for 5–10 minutes before slicing or serving. This helps keep the chicken juicy.

RAJAH
 I N G R E D I E N T S  CHEESE SAUCE
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1¾ cups shredded sharp Cheddar cheese
STUFFED PEPPERS
  • 4 large bell peppers (any color)
  • 2 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 lb ground chicken or turkey
  • 1 tbsp ground chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 can (14 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 1½ cups cooked brown rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice
  • 1¼ cup shredded cheese (Monterey Jack, pepper jack, cheddar, or a mix), divided
SERVING (Optional Toppings)
  • Sliced avocado
  • Chopped fresh cilantro
  • Salsa
  • Sour cream or plain Greek yogurt
  • Freshly squeezed lime juice

1  Make Cheese Sauce: Melt 2 tbsp butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in 2 tbsp flour and ¼ tsp salt until a paste forms. Cook for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in 1 cup whole milk. Cook and stir until the sauce thickens and bubbles, about 2–3 minutes. Remove from heat. Stir in 1¾ cups shredded sharp Cheddar cheese until melted and smooth. Set aside.
2  Preheat oven to 375°F. Lightly coat a 9×13-inch baking dish with nonstick spray.
3  Prepare Peppers: Slice the bell peppers in half lengthwise from top to bottom. Remove the seeds and membranes. Arrange the pepper halves cut side up in the prepared baking dish.
4  Make Filling: Heat 2 tsp olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground chicken (or turkey), chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, ½ tsp kosher salt, and ¼ tsp black pepper. Cook, breaking apart the meat with a spoon, until the chicken is browned and cooked through, about 4–6 minutes. Drain off any excess liquid.
5  Stir in the can of fire-roasted diced tomatoes with their juices. Bring to a simmer and cook for 1 minute.
6  Remove the skillet from the heat. Stir in the cooked rice (or quinoa/cauliflower rice) and ¾ cup of the shredded Monterey Jack (or similar) cheese.
7  Stuff Peppers: Mound the filling evenly inside each bell pepper half. Top with the remaining ½ cup shredded cheese.
8  Pour a small amount of water (about ¼ to ½ cup) into the bottom of the baking dish, around the peppers (not into them). This helps them steam and soften.
9  Bake uncovered for 25 to 35 minutes, or until the peppers are tender and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
10  Serve hot, topped with the prepared cheese sauce and any of your favorite optional toppings.

PEPPER PULLED PORK
Any dark caramel soda will do the job and provide a slightly different taste.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2½-3 lb pork butt/shoulder
  • 750ml Dr. Pepper
  • 1 medium onion, cut into wedges
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1½ tsp dry ground mustard
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
  • 3 tbsp worcestershire sauce
  • barbecue sauce
  • bread rolls or buns

1  Distribute onion in bottom of slow cooker, then place pork on top.
2  Add garlic, mustard, cayenne, salt, pepper, apple cider vinegar, and worcestershire sauce, then pour soda overtop.
3  Cook on high for 4-5 hours, or low for 8.
4  Carefully remove pork and place on cutting board, then shred.
5  Return shredded pork to slow cooker for another hour.
6  Drain remaining juices, then add barbecue sauce to meat and onion mixture to reach desired sauciness.
7  Portion onto toasted buns and serve — pairs well with coleslaw or oven fries!

MEATBALLS
 I N G R E D I E N T S  MEATBALLS
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • ½ lb ground pork
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • ½ cup bread crumbs (plain or panko)
  • 1 egg
  • 5 tbsp whole milk
  • Salt and pepper (to taste)
  • Dash olive oil (for frying meatballs)
SAUCE
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • ⅔ cup vegetable stock
  • ⅔ cup beef stock
  • ⅔ cup thick double cream (heavy cream)
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard

1  Prepare Meatballs: In a large bowl, combine ground beef and ground pork. Break up any large lumps with your fingers.
2  Add finely chopped onion, minced garlic, bread crumbs, and egg. Mix gently but thoroughly.
3  Add milk and season well with salt and pepper. Mix again until just combined (do not overmix).
4  Roll the mixture into small, uniform-sized meatballs. Place on a clean plate, cover, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (or up to 2 hours) to help them hold their shape.
5  Heat a dash of olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Brown the meatballs on all sides, working in batches if necessary to avoid overcrowding the pan. They don't need to be cooked through at this stage.
6  Transfer the browned meatballs to an ovenproof dish. Cover and bake in a preheated oven at 350°F for about 20–30 minutes, or until cooked through.
7  Make the Sauce: While meatballs are baking (or after), melt 3 tbsp of butter in a clean pan or saucepan over medium heat.
8  Whisk in 3 tbsp of flour and cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring constantly, to make a roux.
9  Gradually whisk in the vegetable stock and beef stock, ensuring no lumps form. Continue whisking until the sauce begins to thicken.
10  Stir in the double cream (heavy cream), soy sauce, and Dijon mustard.
11  Bring the sauce to a simmer and cook gently, stirring, until it has thickened to your desired consistency. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
12  Serve the meatballs hot, generously drizzled with the creamy sauce. Often served with mashed potatoes or egg noodles and lingonberry jam.

CHORIZO MAC + CHEESE
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 16 oz elbow macaroni (or other short pasta)
  • 8–12 oz Spanish Chorizo, casing removed, crumbled or diced (adjust to taste)
CHEESE SAUCE
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp dry mustard powder
  • 3 cups milk, warmed
  • 1 cup whipping cream (heavy cream)
  • 4 cups sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1 cup gouda cheese, shredded
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
TOPPING
  • 1½ cups bread crumbs (panko preferred)
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted
  • ½ cup parmesan cheese, shredded
  • ¼ tsp paprika
  • ¼ tsp dried parsley

1  Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a large baking dish (e.g., 9×13 inch).
2  Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the macaroni 1–2 minutes less than package directions for al dente (it will finish cooking in the oven). Drain well.
3  Cook Chorizo: In a large skillet or Dutch oven, cook the crumbled/diced chorizo over medium heat until browned and fat has rendered, about 5–7 minutes. Remove chorizo with a slotted spoon and set aside, leaving about 1–2 tbsp of rendered fat in the skillet (discard excess). If chorizo didn't render much fat, add a little oil or butter.
4  Make Roux: To the same skillet with chorizo fat (or added butter), add 3 tbsp of butter (if needed to make up 3 tbsp total fat). Melt over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and dry mustard powder. Cook, whisking constantly, for about 1–2 minutes until golden and bubbly.
5  Make Cheese Sauce: Gradually whisk in the warmed milk and cream until smooth. Bring to a gentle simmer, whisking frequently, and cook for 2–3 minutes until the sauce begins to thicken. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
6  Remove from heat. Add the shredded cheddar, gouda, and mozzarella cheeses to the sauce. Stir until all cheeses are melted and the sauce is smooth.
7  Combine: Add the cooked pasta and cooked chorizo to the cheese sauce. Stir gently to combine everything well.
8  Pour half of the pasta mixture into the prepared baking dish. If desired, layer with a bit more cheese (optional). Top with the remaining pasta mixture.
9  Prepare Topping: In a medium bowl, combine bread crumbs, melted butter, shredded parmesan cheese, paprika, and dried parsley. Mix well.
10  Sprinkle the breadcrumb topping evenly over the mac and cheese in the baking dish.
11  Bake for 25–30 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the mac and cheese is hot and bubbly.
12  Remove from oven and let rest for 5–10 minutes before serving.

BRAISED POT ROAST
CRITICAL STEP: Sear the roast thoroughly until dark brown. This creates the flavor base for the sauce.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2–3 lb chuck roast
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • plenty of black pepper
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 heaping tbsp pre-minced garlic (or 2 tsp)
  • ½ cup red wine (Cabernet or Merlot, optional; use broth if preferred)
  • 2 cups beef broth (low sodium recommended)
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Fresh thyme and rosemary sprigs (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Large chunks of potatoes (e.g., Yukon Gold)
  • Large chunks of carrots

1  Preheat oven to 300°F. Pat the chuck roast completely dry with paper towels and season generously with salt and pepper on all sides.
2  Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering. Sear the seasoned roast on all sides until a deep, dark brown crust forms (about 3–4 minutes per side). Remove the roast and set aside on a plate.
3  Lower heat to medium. Add chopped onions to the pot and cook for 2–3 minutes until softened. Add the pre-minced garlic and stir for 30 seconds until fragrant (be careful not to burn).
4  Pour in the red wine (if using) or ½ cup of beef broth. Use a wooden spoon to scrape up all the browned bits (fond) from the bottom of the pot. Let it simmer for 2 minutes.
5  Add the remaining beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, rosemary, and bay leaves to the pot. Bring the liquid to a simmer.
6  Place the seared roast back into the Dutch oven, nestled into the liquid. The liquid should come about halfway up the sides of the roast.
7  Cover the Dutch oven tightly with its lid and place it in the preheated oven. Braise for 1 hour and 30 minutes.
8  Carefully remove the Dutch oven from the oven. Turn the roast over in the liquid. Add your large chunks of potatoes and carrots into the liquid around the meat.
9  Cover and return to the oven for another 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until the meat is fork-tender and pulls apart easily.
10  Remove from oven. Let rest, covered, for at least 15 minutes before shredding or slicing and serving.

CHILI
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 lb 90% lean ground beef
  • Optional: ½ pack of bacon, cooked and crumbled (if using, cook mirepoix in reserved bacon grease instead of olive oil)
  • 2½ tbsp chili powder
  • 2 tbsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • ¼ tsp ground cayenne pepper (optional, or to taste)
  • Optional additions: Sweet paprika, onion powder, dried oregano (to taste, complementing above spices)
  • 1½ cups beef broth
  • 1 (15 oz) can petite diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 1 (16 oz) can red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce

1  If using bacon, cook it in a large soup pot or Dutch oven until crispy. Remove bacon, crumble, and set aside. Reserve 1 tbsp of bacon grease in the pot (discard extra) or use 1 tbsp olive oil.
2  Heat oil (or reserved bacon grease) in the pot over medium-high heat for about 2 minutes.
3  Add diced onion (and other mirepoix vegetables like celery or bell pepper if using). Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.
4  Add ground beef to the pot. Break it apart with a spoon. Cook for 6–7 minutes, or until browned, stirring occasionally. Drain off any excess fat if necessary.
5  Add chili powder, cumin, sugar, tomato paste, garlic powder, salt, pepper, cayenne (if using), and any other desired spices (paprika, onion powder, oregano). Stir well to combine with the meat and onions, and cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
6  Pour in beef broth, undrained diced tomatoes, drained and rinsed kidney beans, and tomato sauce. Stir well to combine. If using, add the crumbled bacon back to the pot.
7  Bring the chili to a low boil. Then, reduce the heat to low or medium-low to maintain a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for at least 20–25 minutes (or longer, up to several hours, for more flavor development), stirring occasionally.
8  Remove the pot from the heat. Let the chili rest for 5–10 minutes before serving. This allows the flavors to meld further. Serve with your favorite toppings (cheese, sour cream, onions, etc.).

STEW
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 lb stewing beef, cut into 1–2 inch cubes
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 6 cups beef broth
  • ½ cup red wine (optional, can substitute with more broth)
  • 1 lb potatoes, peeled and cubed (e.g., Russet, Yukon Gold)
  • 4 carrots, peeled and cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 4 stalks celery, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary (or 1 fresh sprig, remove before serving)
SLURRY (optional, to thicken)
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water
  • ¾ cup frozen peas

1  In a bowl, combine flour, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Add beef cubes and toss to coat evenly.
2  Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear the coated beef in batches until browned on all sides. Do not overcrowd the pot. Remove browned beef and set aside.
3  Add chopped onion to the same pot. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes. If using red wine, pour it in and deglaze the pot by scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Let wine simmer for a minute.
4  Return the seared beef to the pot. Add beef broth, cubed potatoes, carrots, celery, tomato paste, and rosemary. Stir to combine.
5  Bring the stew to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and cook for 1 to 1.5 hours, or until the beef is tender. Stir occasionally.
6  If a thicker stew is desired, make a slurry: In a small bowl, mix cornstarch with cold water until smooth. Slowly stir the slurry into the simmering stew. Cook for a few more minutes, stirring, until the stew has thickened.
7  Stir in frozen peas. Simmer for an additional 5–10 minutes, or until peas are heated through.
8  Taste and season with additional salt and pepper if needed. Remove rosemary sprig if using fresh. Serve hot.

COTTAGE PIE
Cool the filling before adding the mash so the potato doesn't sink. For a quick cool-down, put it in the freezer while you make the potatoes.

 I N G R E D I E N T S  FILLING
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 1 celery, finely chopped
  • 1½ lb beef mince (ground beef)
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cup tomato paste
  • 2 cups beef stock/broth, low sodium
  • ½ cup red wine (or water)
  • 1 beef bouillon cube, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme (or 3 sprigs fresh thyme)
  • 2 dried bay leaves
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
TOPPING
  • 2½ lb potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • ⅔ cup milk, warmed
  • 2 tbsp butter
PARMESAN CRUST (Optional)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp parmesan, grated

1  Heat oil in a large skillet over medium high heat. Add onion and garlic, cook for 1 minute. Then add carrots and celery. Cook for 3 minutes or until softened and sweet.
2  Turn heat up to high. Add beef and cook, breaking it up as you go, until browned.
3  Add flour and mix in. Add tomato paste, broth, red wine, bouillon cube, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, bay leaves, salt and pepper.
4  Bring to simmer, then turn down heat so it is simmering rapidly. Cook for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it reduces down to a thick gravy consistency. Taste then add more salt if desired.
5  Transfer to a 1.5 L / 6 cup pie dish. It's best to let the filling cool to make topping easier.
6  Assemble Pie: Preheat oven to 350°F.
7  Cook potatoes in boiling water for 15 minutes or until soft. Drain, then return to the pot on the turned-off stove to steam dry for about 30 seconds.
8  Add butter to the potatoes and mash until melted, then add milk and salt. Mash until smooth.
9  Spread the hot mash onto the pie filling. Use a fork to rough up the surface for more crispy bits. Sprinkle with parmesan and drizzle with butter (if using the optional crust).
10  Bake for 25–30 minutes or until golden on top and bubbling on the edges. Check that the center is piping hot.
11  Let stand for 5 minutes before serving, garnished with fresh thyme leaves if desired.

SAUSAGE RAGÙ
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1 carrot, peeled and grated
  • 2 celery stalks, grated
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 12 oz pork sausages, meat removed from casings
  • 12 oz beef sausages, meat removed from casings
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup pinot noir red wine (or 0% alcohol red wine/extra chicken stock)
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken stock/broth
  • 14 oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 3 thyme sprigs (or ½ tsp dried)
  • 2 bay leaves (fresh or dried)
  • ½ tsp cooking salt / kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp chilli flakes (red pepper flakes), optional
  • 1 lb pappardelle pasta (for serving)
  • Parmesan/Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated (for serving)
  • Parsley, finely chopped (optional garnish)

1  Preheat oven to 350°F.
2  Soffritto: Heat olive oil in a large, oven-safe pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion, garlic, grated carrot, grated celery, and fennel seeds. Cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are soft and sweet, but not browned.
3  Cook Sausage: Turn heat up to high. Add the sausage meat (removed from casings) to the pot. Cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until it's no longer pink. It doesn't need to brown deeply.
4  Deglaze: Add tomato paste and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly. Pour in the red wine, bring to a simmer, and cook for 2 minutes, scraping any browned bits from the bottom of the pot, until the wine has reduced by about half.
5  Slow Cook #1 (Oven): Add chicken stock, crushed tomatoes, thyme sprigs, bay leaves, salt, pepper, and chilli flakes (if using). Stir well. Bring to a simmer on the stovetop, then cover the pot with a lid and transfer to the preheated oven. Cook for 1 hour.
6  Slow Cook #2 (Oven): Remove from oven. If desired, use a potato masher or sturdy spoon to break up the sausage meat further. Return to the oven, uncovered, for another 45 minutes to 1 hour, or until the sauce has thickened and deepened in flavor.
7  Season: Remove from oven. Remove bay leaves and thyme sprigs. Taste the sauce and add more salt if needed (sausages vary in saltiness).
8  Cook Pasta: While the sauce is finishing its last 30 minutes in the oven, cook pappardelle pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Just before draining, reserve about 1 to 1½ cups of the pasta cooking water. Drain the pasta.
9  Toss with Sauce: Add the drained pasta directly to the pot with the bolognese sauce. Add a splash (about ½ cup) of the reserved pasta water. Toss everything together over low heat until the pasta is well-coated with the sauce and the sauce clings to the strands. Add more pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce to your desired consistency.
10  Serve: Serve immediately in warmed bowls, garnished generously with finely grated Parmesan or Parmigiano Reggiano and a pinch of finely chopped parsley, if desired.

TACO-STUFFED PEPPERS
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 4 large bell peppers (any color), tops cut off, seeds and membranes removed
  • 1 lb lean ground beef (or turkey)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup cooked long grain or wild rice
  • 1 cup sweet corn (canned, drained, or frozen, thawed)
  • 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes (canned or fresh, for filling)
  • 1 packet taco seasoning (or make your own: chili powder, cumin, salt, pepper, paprika, oregano)
  • Water (as per taco seasoning instructions, usually ¼ to ½ cup)
  • 1½–2 cups shredded cheese (e.g., Monterey Jack, Cheddar, Mexican blend), divided
TOPPING (Pico de Gallo style)
  • 1 cup additional diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 small white onion, finely chopped (or red onion)
  • Optional: Lime juice

1  Preheat oven to 375°F.
2  Place the prepared bell peppers (hollowed out) cut-side up in a baking dish. You can par-bake them for 10–15 minutes to soften them slightly, or stuff them raw (they will be more crisp-tender).
3  Prepare Filling: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and cook, breaking it up with a spatula, until browned and cooked through (about 5–7 minutes). Drain any excess fat.
4  Add minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds more until fragrant.

6  Remove from heat. Stir in the cooked rice, corn, black beans, 1 cup diced tomatoes (for filling), and about 1 to 1½ cups of the shredded cheese. Mix well to combine.
7  Stuff Peppers: Divide the meat and rice mixture evenly among the hollowed-out bell peppers, packing it in gently.
8  Top each stuffed pepper with the remaining shredded cheese.
9  Pour a little water (about ½ cup) into the bottom of the baking dish around the peppers (not into them) to help them steam.
10  Bake for 20–30 minutes (if peppers were par-baked) or 30–40 minutes (if stuffed raw), until the peppers are tender and the filling is heated through and cheese is melted and bubbly.
11  Prepare Topping: While peppers are baking, combine the additional diced tomatoes, chopped cilantro, and finely chopped white/red onion in a small bowl. Add a squeeze of lime juice if desired.
12  Remove peppers from oven and let them rest for a few minutes. Top with the fresh tomato-onion-cilantro mixture before serving.

FIRECRACKER SHRIMP
TECHNIQUE: Maintain oil at 350°F. If it drops too low, the coating will be greasy rather than crispy.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sweet chili sauce
  • ¼ cup sriracha (adjust to taste)
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined, tail on or off
  • ½ cup cornstarch
  • 1–2 cups flavorless oil (e.g., canola, vegetable for frying)
  • 2 cups mixed greens or lettuce
  • 2 green onions, chopped (for garnish)

1  Make Sauce: In a small bowl, mix together mayonnaise, sweet chili sauce, sriracha, and salt. Set aside. Adjust sriracha for desired heat level.
2  Prepare for Frying: Heat oil in a deep pan or Dutch oven to 350°F. Ensure there's enough oil to at least halfway submerge the shrimp, about 1–2 inches deep.
3  Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels. Place cornstarch in a shallow dish or bowl. Coat the shrimp thoroughly in cornstarch, shaking off any excess.
4  Fry Shrimp: Working in small batches to avoid overcrowding the pan (which lowers oil temperature), carefully add the coated shrimp to the hot oil. Fry for 2–3 minutes, or until golden brown and cooked through. Shrimp cook quickly.
5  Remove fried shrimp with a slotted spoon and drain on a wire rack or paper towel-lined plate.
6  Toss and Serve: In a large bowl, toss the fried shrimp with the prepared firecracker sauce until evenly coated.
7  Arrange mixed greens or lettuce in a serving bowl or on plates. Top with the sauced shrimp.
8  Garnish with chopped green onions and serve immediately.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • ½ cup butter (or ¼ cup butter + ¼ cup oil)
  • 2 yellow onions, chopped
  • 4 stalks celery, chopped
  • 5 green onions, chopped (whites and greens separated)
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 serrano chili pepper, chopped (optional, for heat)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp salt (or to taste)
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • ½ tbsp black pepper
  • ½ tbsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
  • ½ tbsp dried oregano
  • ½ tbsp dried thyme
  • 1½ lb Andouille sausage (or other smoked sausage), cut into ½ inch slices
  • Optional: 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breast, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • Optional: 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 29 oz (approx. 3½ cups) chicken broth
  • 1 can (14½ oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 3 cups long-grain white rice, rinsed

8  Once rice is cooked, remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 10 minutes.
9  Fluff with a fork. Stir in the green parts of the green onions. Remove bay leaves before serving. Adjust seasoning if needed.

BANANA BREAD
Frozen bananas create extra liquid, but you can microwave fresh ones to artificially ripen them.

 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 6 ripe bananas, peeled
  • 8 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 2 large eggs
  • ¾ cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tsp granulated sugar

1  Adjust oven rack to middle position, preheat oven to 350°F, and grease a 9x5" loaf pan.
2  In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
3  Place bananas in a strainer set over a bowl and let it drain, stirring occasionally, for 15 minutes.
4  Transfer collected liquid to a medium saucepan and cook over medium-high heat until it reduces to a ¼ cup — about 5 minutes.
5  Return bananas to bowl, mash in reduced liquid until mostly smooth, then whisk in melted butter, eggs, brown sugar, and vanilla.
6  Pour banana mixture into flour mixture, stir until just combined, then transfer batter into prepared pan.
7  Sprinkle granulated sugar evenly over loaf, then bake until skewer inserted in center of loaf comes out clean — about 55 minutes to 1¼ hour, rotating halfway through baking.
8  Let loaf cool in pan for 10 minutes, then remove and let cool on wire rack for 1 hour before serving.

GRANDMA'S APPLE PIE
 I N G R E D I E N T S  PIE CRUST (makes 2 crusts)
  • 2½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, very cold, cut into cubes
  • ½ cup ice water, or more as needed
APPLE FILLING
  • 6–8 medium apples (Granny Smith, Honeycrisp, or a mix), peeled, cored, and sliced ¼-inch thick
  • ½ cup granulated sugar (adjust to apple sweetness)
  • ¼ cup packed light brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (or 1 tbsp cornstarch)
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
EGG WASH (optional)
  • 1 large egg, beaten with 1 tbsp water or milk

1  Make the Pie Crust: In a large bowl, whisk together flour and salt. Add cold, cubed butter. Cut in butter using a pastry blender or your fingertips until mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces of butter remaining.
2  Gradually add ice water, one tbsp at a time, mixing lightly with a fork after each addition, until dough just comes together. Be careful not to add too much water.
3  Divide dough in half, flatten each half into a disc, wrap in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (or up to 2 days).
4  Prepare Oven and Pie Plate: Preheat oven to 400°F. Place an oven rack in the lower third of the oven.
5  Prepare the Filling: In a large bowl, combine sliced apples, granulated sugar, brown sugar, 2 tbsp flour (for filling), cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice. Toss gently to coat evenly.
6  Assemble the Pie: On a lightly floured surface, roll out one disc of chilled dough into a 12-inch circle. Carefully transfer to a 9-inch pie plate. Trim edges to leave about a 1-inch overhang.
7  Pour the apple filling into the crust-lined pie plate. Dot the top of the filling with the 2 tbsp of butter pieces.
8  Roll out the second disc of dough into an 11 or 12-inch circle. Place over the filling. (Alternatively, cut into strips for a lattice top).
9  Trim the top crust edges to match the bottom crust overhang. Seal the edges by crimping them together with your fingers or a fork. Cut several vents in the top crust to allow steam to escape.
10  If using, brush the top crust with the beaten egg wash.
11  Bake the Pie: Bake at 400°F for 20–25 minutes.
12  Reduce oven temperature to 375°F. Continue baking for another 30–40 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly. If the crust edges start to brown too quickly, cover them with foil or a pie shield.
13  Let the pie cool on a wire rack for at least 2–3 hours (preferably 4 hours) before slicing and serving. This allows the filling to set.

BLUEBERRY MOJITO PIE
 I N G R E D I E N T S  PIE CRUST (makes 2 crusts)
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 1 cup vegetable shortening, chilled and cut into pieces
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tbsp ice water
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
BLUEBERRY MOJITO FILLING
  • 5 cups blueberries (fresh or frozen, if frozen do not thaw)
  • 2 limes, zested and juiced (about ¼ cup juice)
  • ⅓ cup white sugar (adjust to blueberry sweetness)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh mint
FINISHING
  • 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash, optional)
  • Demerara sugar for sprinkling (optional)

1  Make Pie Crust: In a large bowl, combine flour and ¾ tsp salt. Cut in chilled shortening using a pastry blender or your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces.
2  In a small bowl, beat 1 egg, 2 tbsp ice water, and 1 tbsp white vinegar together. Pour this liquid mixture over the flour mixture. Stir with a fork until the dough just comes together.
3  Divide dough in half, form each half into a disc, wrap in plastic, and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes (or up to 2 days).
4  Make Filling: In a medium saucepan, combine 2 cups of the blueberries, lime juice, lime zest, white sugar, and ½ tsp salt. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, for about 5–7 minutes, until blueberries start to burst and release their juices.
5  In a small bowl, whisk together cornstarch and 2 tbsp water until smooth. Stir this slurry into the berry mixture in the saucepan. Cook, stirring constantly, for 1–2 minutes more, until the mixture thickens and bubbles.
6  Remove from heat. Gently stir in the remaining 3 cups of blueberries and the chopped fresh mint. Let the filling cool completely while you prepare the crusts.
7  Assemble Pie: Preheat oven to 400°F. Place a large baking sheet on the middle rack to preheat (this helps create a crispy bottom crust).
8  On a lightly floured surface, roll out one disc of chilled dough into a 12-inch circle. Carefully transfer to a 9-inch pie plate. Trim edges if necessary.
9  Pour the cooled blueberry mojito filling into the crust-lined pie plate.
10  Roll out the second disc of dough. Place it over the filling as a solid top crust (cut vents) or cut into strips for a lattice top. Trim, seal, and crimp the edges.
11  If desired, brush the top crust with beaten egg wash and sprinkle with Demerara sugar.
12  Place the pie in the freezer for 15–20 minutes to chill the crust thoroughly (helps prevent shrinking).
13  Bake Pie: Place the chilled pie on the preheated baking sheet in the oven. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes.
14  Reduce oven temperature to 375°F and continue baking for another 30–40 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling. If the crust edges brown too quickly, cover them with foil or a pie shield.
15  Let the pie cool on a wire rack for at least 2–3 hours (preferably 4 hours) before cutting and serving, to allow the filling to set.

CINNAMON ROLLS
 I N G R E D I E N T S  DOUGH
  • 2¼ tsp active dry yeast (1 standard packet)
  • 1 cup warm milk (105–115°F)
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • ⅓ cup butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 4–4½ cups all-purpose flour (plus more for dusting)
FILLING
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • ⅓ cup unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
CREAM CHEESE ICING
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 1½ cups powdered sugar (icing sugar), sifted
  • ¼ cup (2 oz) cream cheese, at room temperature
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ⅛ tsp salt (or a pinch)

1  Make Dough: In a large bowl (or bowl of a stand mixer), dissolve yeast in warm milk. Let sit for 5–10 minutes until frothy.
2  Add granulated sugar, melted butter, salt, and eggs to the yeast mixture. Whisk to combine.
3  Gradually add 4 cups of flour. If using a stand mixer, use the dough hook and mix on low speed until a soft dough forms. If mixing by hand, stir until combined, then turn out onto a lightly floured surface.
4  Knead the dough for 5–7 minutes (by mixer or hand) until smooth and elastic. Add more flour, 1 tbsp at a time, only if the dough is too sticky to handle. Dough should be slightly tacky but pull away from the sides of the bowl.
5  Place dough in a lightly oiled bowl, turning to coat. Cover with a clean, damp towel or plastic wrap. Let rise in a warm place for 1 to 1.5 hours, or until doubled in size.
6  Prepare Filling & Pan: While dough rises, combine brown sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl. Generously butter a 9×13 inch baking dish.
7  Assemble Rolls: Punch down the risen dough. Turn out onto a lightly floured surface and roll into a rectangle approximately 16×12 inches and about ¼ inch thick.
8  Spread the ⅓ cup softened butter evenly over the dough, leaving a small (½ inch) border along one long edge.
9  Sprinkle the brown sugar-cinnamon mixture evenly over the buttered dough.
10  Starting from one long edge (the one without the border), roll up the dough tightly into a log. Pinch the seam (the border edge) to seal.
11  Cut the log into 12 equal 1½-inch sections. A serrated knife or unflavored dental floss works well.
12  Place the rolls cut-side up in the prepared baking dish.
13  Second Rise (Proofing): Cover the dish loosely with plastic wrap or a damp towel and let the rolls rise in a warm place for 30–45 minutes, or until nearly doubled in size.
14  Bake: Preheat oven to 350°F during the last 15 minutes of proofing. Bake the rolls for 20–25 minutes, or until golden brown.
15  Make Icing: While rolls are baking (or cooling), beat softened butter, sifted powdered sugar, room temperature cream cheese, vanilla extract, and salt in a medium bowl with an electric mixer until smooth and fluffy.
16  Let the baked cinnamon rolls cool in the pan for 5–10 minutes before spreading generously with the cream cheese icing. Serve warm.

SHORTBREAD COOKIES
 I N G R E D I E N T S 
  • 2 cups (4 sticks) unsalted butter, softened (not melted)
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar (or granulated sugar for a crisper cookie)
  • 4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Sprinkles (optional)

2  In a large bowl, cream together softened butter and brown sugar with an electric mixer until light and fluffy. Stir in vanilla extract.

8  Bake for 15–25 minutes (time depends on thickness and oven temperature), or until the edges are very lightly golden. Shortbread should not brown much.
9  Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5–10 minutes before carefully transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely. They will be delicate when warm.
